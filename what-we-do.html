What We Do

The case types we take, and what to expect from working with us.

Divorce

Filing for divorce, negotiating separation agreements, and calculating spousal or child support. We handle both uncontested filings and more contested proceedings.

Debt & Collections Defense

Responding to a collections lawsuit, negotiating settlements with creditors, disputing inaccurate debts, and stopping wage garnishment before it starts.

How a case moves forward

From intake to resolution, here's what the process generally looks like.

  • 1. Intake

    You submit the intake form. A coordinator reviews it and confirms your case fits our scope.

  • 2. Initial consultation

    You meet with a Allied Legal Professional and supervising attorney to go over your situation and options.

  • 3. Prepare, Guide, Inform

    If we take your situation , we give advice on your next steps and help with any documents you may need to continue your pro-se representation.

  • 4. Resolution & follow-up

    Once the matter is closed , we are always available if related issues come up later.
