Who We Are

The people behind the clinic, and how we're organized.

A team of attorneys, students, and advocates

The clinic is staffed by supervising attorneys with decades of combined experience in family and consumer law, alongside law students who handle casework under direct supervision as part of their clinical training.

Every case is reviewed by a licensed attorney before any filing, negotiation, or court appearance. Students bring energy and up-to-date research; attorneys bring judgment and courtroom experience.

How we're organized

  • Supervising Attorneys

    Licensed attorneys who review and sign off on every case.

  • Clinical Law Students

    Handle client meetings, drafting, and research under supervision.

  • Intake Coordinators

    The first point of contact — they review your intake form and route your case.

  • Community Partners

    Local nonprofits and legal aid organizations we refer to and work alongside.

Where we come from

The clinic was founded to give law students hands-on experience while filling a real gap in access to family and consumer legal help in our community.

Rooted in the community

Most of our clients are referred by local shelters, schools, and social services — organizations that see these problems first.

Accountable to our clients

We track outcomes, not just case counts, and adjust how we work based on client feedback each term.
