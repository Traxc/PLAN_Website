Who We Are

The people behind the clinic, and how we're organized.

A team of attorneys, Allied Legal Professionals and partners

PLAN is an pilot law clinic. We are tasked by the Indiana Supreme Court to create a new legal license that lowers the barriers to legal help. Future license holders will inform, prepare, and guide Hoosiers through their legal issues at the lowest possible cost. We are holding free legal consultations to train the first generation of new legal practitioners.

The clinic is staffed by supervising attorneys with experience in family and consumer law, alongside Allied Legal Professionals who handle matters under direct supervision as part of their clinical training.

How we're organized

  • Supervising Attorneys

    Licensed attorneys who review and sign off on every matter.

  • Allied Legal Professionals

    A brand new profession geared towards dealing with your daily legal hassels

  • Community Partners

    Local nonprofits and legal aid organizations we refer to and work alongside.

Where we come from

The clinic was founded to give Allied Legal Professionals hands-on experience while filling a real gap in access to family and consumer legal help in our community.

Rooted in the community

Most of our clients are referred by local shelters, schools, and social services organizations that see these problems first.

Accountable to our clients

We track outcomes, not just counts, and adjust how we work based on client feedback each term.
