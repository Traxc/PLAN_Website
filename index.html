Where we help

Our attorneys and Allied Legal Professionals take cases in four core areas.

Divorce

Uncontested and contested filings, separation agreements, and support calculations.

Debt Defense

Responding to collection lawsuits, negotiating settlements, and stopping wage garnishment.

Legal Document Support

Reviewing and helping prepare court forms, agreements, notices, and other documents. We explain what they mean before you sign or file them.

Referrals

When a case falls outside our scope, we connect clients with vetted legal aid partners.
