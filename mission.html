Request Legal Help

Legal help shouldn't depend on what you can afford.

Family separation, a collections lawsuit, or a predatory loan can unravel a household fast and the legal system that's supposed to offer a way through it is often confusing, slow, and expensive to access. We exist to close that gap.

Our mission is to inform, prepare, and guide Hoosiers through the legal process. We leverage a high-volume of everyday legal issues to deliver assistance for the lowest cost.

What guides our work

  • Access first

    No client is turned away solely because of ability to pay.

  • Plain language

    Every client understands their options before deciding anything.

  • Dignity, not case numbers

    We treat every matter as a person's life, not a docket entry.

Divorce

Filing for divorce, negotiating separation agreements, and calculating spousal or child support. We handle both uncontested filings and more contested proceedings.

Debt & Collections Defense

Responding to a collections lawsuit, negotiating settlements with creditors, disputing inaccurate debts, and stopping wage garnishment before it starts.

How a case moves forward

From intake to resolution, here's what the process generally looks like.

  • 1. Intake

    You submit the intake form. A coordinator reviews it and confirms your case fits our scope.

  • 2. Initial consultation

    You meet with a legal professional and supervising attorney to go over your situation and options.

  • 3. Prepare, Guide, Inform

    If we take your matter, we explain your next steps and help with documents you may need to continue representing yourself.

  • 4. Resolution & follow-up

    Once the matter is closed, we remain available if related issues come up later.
