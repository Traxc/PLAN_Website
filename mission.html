Our Mission

Why the clinic exists, and who it's meant to serve.

Legal help shouldn't depend on what you can afford.

Family separation, a collections lawsuit, or a predatory loan can unravel a household fast — and the legal system that's supposed to offer a way through it is often confusing, slow, and expensive to access. We exist to close that gap.

The PLAN’s mission is to inform, prepare, and guide Hoosiers through the legal process. We leverage a high-volume of everyday legal issues to deliver assistance for the lowest cost

What guides our work

  • Access first

    No client is turned away solely because of ability to pay.

  • Plain language

    Every client understands their options before deciding anything.

  • Dignity, not case numbers

    We treat every matter as a person's life, not a docket entry.

A fair outcome shouldn't require a full-price attorney to get one.
